# Reddit post package

## Title

[OC] Reported body fat vs BMI for 2 owners of the same smart scale, 41 readings

## Body

The animation follows my 37 GE CS10H readings over 3 months, then switches to 4 readings from a 6′8″ owner of the same model. Both sets follow the same 1.5 × BMI formula, with the other owner’s line shifted 0.95 percentage point lower.

Full data and method: https://aiandtractors.com/ge-cs10h-body-fat-formula/

## Source and tools comment

Source: 37 readings from my Fit Profile export, plus 4 values digitized from u/WorldTallestEngineer’s replication chart using the same GE CS10H. I calculated BMI from my measured weight and 1.86 m profile height. The reviewer reported a 2.032 m height.

My readings fit body fat % = 1.50 × BMI − 17.50. The other owner’s readings fit body fat % = 1.50 × BMI − 18.45, with a mean error of 0.025 percentage point and a worst error of 0.05 point.

My 3 barefoot figures represent my recorded weight range. The anonymous figure encodes only the other owner’s reported height and approximate BMI. Every plotted dot is data.

Tools: JavaScript, SVG, Sharp, and FFmpeg.

Peer chart: https://www.reddit.com/r/dataisugly/comments/1w18i27/comment/p6jikqv/
